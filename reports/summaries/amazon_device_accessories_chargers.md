# Amazon Device Accessories & Chargers

## Buying Guide

## Overview

This category contains Amazon device chargers and accessories, covering 532 total reviews across 2 ranked products. The evidence is based on 211 and 188 reviews respectively, providing solid sample sizes for comparison. Note that about 25% of reviews in this category lack clear product identification, which may affect overall category insights.

## Top Products

**Amazon 5W USB Official OEM Charger And Power Adapter For Fire Tablets And Kindle Ereaders**
- Average Rating: 4.45 stars
- Positive Reviews: 86.7%

Buyers praise this charger for quick charging speeds and compact design. Reviewers note it "charges the kindle really quickly" and highlight "good quality." Users appreciate its portability and report it charges devices faster than laptop USB ports.

**All New Kindle E Reader Black 6 Glare Free Touchscreen Display Wi-Fi Includes Special Offers**
- Average Rating: 4.43 stars
- Positive Reviews: 84.8%

This device receives slightly lower ratings than the charger. Buyers praise its convenience, with reviewers noting it allows charging "without the need of computer being on" and appreciate having charging options when traveling. However, some positive reviews mention cheaper alternatives are available elsewhere.

The difference between these products is small—only 0.02 stars separate them. Both maintain strong positive ratings above 84%.

## Main Complaints

**Amazon 5W USB Charger**
The main complaint theme is pricing, appearing in roughly 6 of the 10 sampled low-rated reviews. Buyers feel the $20 price is excessive for a basic USB adapter, with one reviewer noting "5 dollar AC to USB power adapter for 20 buck." Some buyers questioned whether a purchase was necessary at all. Additional complaints include a missing cable and lack of color options.

**Kindle E Reader**
Price complaints dominate this product too, appearing in about 7 of the 10 sampled low-rated reviews. Buyers argue the charger "should have come with the kindle" at no extra cost and feel "it should not have had to buy it." Some reviewers claim the advertising was misleading about whether a plug is included. One sample review also reported that charging performance degraded over time.

## Weakest Product

The Kindle E Reader is the weakest product with 4.43 stars and 8.1% negative reviews, compared to the charger's 4.45 stars and 10.6% negative reviews. However, the difference is minimal. Both products face similar pricing objections from dissatisfied buyers.

## Recommendation

Choose the **Amazon 5W USB Charger** if you need a dedicated, fast-charging adapter specifically optimized for Kindle devices. Select the **Kindle E Reader** if you want a complete e-reading device. Both products receive strong ratings, but buyers across both should consider third-party alternatives, as reviewers note comparable products are available at lower prices.
